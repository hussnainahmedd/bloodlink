## The Problem in Pakistan

Blood is needed every single day in Pakistan — road accidents, surgeries, maternity emergencies, dengue outbreaks, cancer treatment — yet finding a donor in time is chaotic:

- Requests circulate as forwarded WhatsApp messages and Facebook posts. Numbers are dead, details are missing, and nobody knows which requests are still open.
- There is no single, searchable place to find a willing donor with the right blood group in your city.
- Donors who *want* to help are never notified when someone near them needs their blood group.
- Rare groups (AB−, B−, O−) are nearly impossible to find in an emergency.
- Thalassemia patients need regular transfusions for life and depend on finding repeat donors again and again.
- Hospitals and blood banks are not connected to any shared donor network.

**The result:** families lose precious hours calling around while a patient waits.

## The Idea

**BloodLink** — a platform built specifically for Pakistan that connects blood donors with patients, families, hospitals, and blood banks in real time, by blood group and city.

It is always **free for patients**. Always.

## How It Will Work in Pakistan

### For donors
1. Register once: name, blood group, city + area, mobile number / WhatsApp, and last donation date.
2. Verify your number with an OTP so requests reach real people.
3. Set yourself **available** or **unavailable**. After donating, the app reminds you when you're eligible again (~3 months).
4. When a matching request appears in your city, you get an instant alert on WhatsApp/SMS.
5. Accept, and only then is your number shared with the requester. Your privacy is protected until you say yes.

### For patients and families
1. Post a blood request in under a minute: blood group, units needed, city, hospital, contact number, and urgency level.
2. BloodLink instantly finds **compatible donors** nearby (e.g. an O+ request also matches O− donors) and alerts them.
3. The request appears on a public **open request board** so anyone browsing can help.
4. The request can be shared as a ready-made formatted WhatsApp message — because that's how Pakistan actually spreads urgent news.
5. Mark the request **fulfilled** once blood is arranged, so donors stop getting alerts.

### For hospitals and blood banks
- Verified accounts (e.g. hospitals in Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi) can post official requests.
- Blood banks can see demand patterns by city and blood group.
- Partnerships with thalassemia foundations and NGOs for recurring donor programs.

## Built for Pakistan, Not Just Translated

- **WhatsApp-first** — alerts and request sharing through the app Pakistan actually uses, not just email.
- **Urdu + English interface** from day one (regional languages later).
- **Low-bandwidth friendly** — must work on basic smartphones with weak signals, because emergencies don't wait for 4G.
- **City-first launch** — start with Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Lahore, and Karachi, then expand city by city.
- **University blood drives** — campus chapters (starting with universities like Air University) running donation camps linked to the platform.
- **Emergency mode** — critical requests broadcast to *all* compatible donors in the city, not just a few.

## Planned Features

**Phase 1 — MVP**
- Donor registration with OTP verification
- Post a blood request (under a minute)
- Search compatible donors by blood group + city
- Public open request board
- Mark requests fulfilled
- One-tap "Share on WhatsApp" for every request

**Phase 2**
- Automatic WhatsApp/SMS alerts to nearby matching donors
- Urdu interface
- Donor availability toggle + eligibility reminders
- Hospital and blood bank verified accounts
- Request urgency levels and expiry

**Phase 3**
- Mobile apps (Android first — that's what Pakistan uses)
- Recurring donor matching for thalassemia patients
- Donor badges and verified-donor trust system
- Analytics dashboard for blood banks and NGOs
- NGO partnerships (thalassemia foundations, Edhi-style welfare networks)

## Trust & Safety

- Phone numbers verified by OTP before anyone can post or donate.
- Donor contact details stay hidden until the donor accepts a request.
- Fake/duplicate requests can be reported and removed.
- No selling of blood — the platform is strictly voluntary and non-commercial.
